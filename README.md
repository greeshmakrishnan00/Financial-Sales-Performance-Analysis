# 📊 Financial Sales Performance Analysis

### Turning Financial Sales Data into Meaningful Business Insights

A data analytics project focused on analyzing financial sales performance, profitability, product trends, and customer segments using **Python, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and Power BI**.

This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, statistical analysis, visualizations, and an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a financial sales dataset to understand business performance across different products, states, customer segments, and time periods.

The analysis focuses on identifying top-performing products, evaluating profitability, understanding customer segment contributions, and exploring monthly sales trends.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to present key performance indicators (KPIs) and business insights in a clear and user-friendly format.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and states.
- Compare sales and profitability across customer segments.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Analyze monthly sales trends and year-over-year changes.
- Evaluate units sold, manufacturing costs, and selling prices.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection** – Loaded the financial sales dataset containing product, sales, profit, state, segment, and date information.
2. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing** – Checked missing values, duplicate records, data types, and data consistency using Python.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – Performed statistical analysis and explored patterns in sales, profit, discounts, and units sold.
4. **Business Analysis** – Compared product performance, state-wise sales, customer segments, and monthly trends.
5. **Data Visualization** – Created charts and visualizations to communicate key findings.
6. **Dashboard Development** – Built an interactive Power BI dashboard using KPIs, charts, and filters.
7. **Insights & Reporting** – Summarized findings to support business performance evaluation and decision-making.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- **Top-Selling Product:** Smart TV generated the highest total sales of approximately 329,445.
- **Highest-Sales State:** Goa recorded the highest total sales of approximately 324,507.
- **Top Revenue Segment:** Wholesale contributed approximately 35.25% of total sales.
- **Highest Units Sold:** Wholesale recorded 20,269 units sold.
- **Sales Growth:** Tablets experienced the largest sales increase between 2013 and 2014, rising by approximately 174,529.
- **Profitability:** Kerala recorded the highest profit-to-COGS ratio among the analyzed states at approximately 168.01%.
- **Sales & Units Relationship:** Units sold and sales showed a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.975.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** October 2014 recorded the highest monthly sales in the analyzed period.
- **Discount & Profit Analysis:** Discounts showed a very weak negative correlation with profit in the dataset.
